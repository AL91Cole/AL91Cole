## Featured work

### 🧪 Cole Learning Labs

A free, browser-based technology learning platform I designed and built for K–12 students, educators, families, and adult beginners. Learners play the school tech team: read a ticket, follow the clues, repair simulated equipment or settings, test the result, and explain whether the original task works.

- **100 activities** across hardware and service desk, networking and operating systems, AI literacy and audit, ethical hacking and penetration testing, and a bounded JavaScript/Python coding sandbox.
- **Privacy-first by default.** Learner work stays on the device. AI literacy labs use authored examples and local rule checks, with no live AI API. Optional cloud replies are off until a learner explicitly turns them on, and even then only an authored scenario ID leaves the browser.
- **Accessible on purpose.** WCAG 2.2 AA targets, a Display menu for text size, spacing, contrast, and reduced motion, a Listen control, keyboard and touch alternatives for every physical interaction, and structured HTML alternatives for every PDF.
- **Built for real classrooms.** Grade-band starting points, progressive coaching clues that never do the work for the student, hypothesis-test reports, and a published AI-use notice for district review.

**Explore:** [colelearninglabs.com](https://www.colelearninglabs.com) · [View the repository](https://github.com/AL91Cole/cole-learning-labs)

### 🛡️ AI Guardian Web Shield

A published, privacy-first Chrome extension that analyzes webpage, link, and form signals locally and turns them into calm, plain-language risk guidance.

- Recognizes phishing indicators, suspicious links, lookalike domains, risky sign-in forms, and manipulative urgency.
- Connected form-action analysis warns when credentials could leak through a GET submission, an unencrypted destination, or a changed origin, without ever reading field values.
- Zero runtime dependencies, a no-network rule enforced in CI, and honest documentation of what the extension cannot see.

**Explore:** [Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ipledehmklfhhdpobeogfdhmhdhmnden) · [View the repository](https://github.com/AL91Cole/ai-guardian-web-shield)

### 🔐 AI Guardian Assistant — Project Labyrinth

A defensive Zero Trust simulation that evaluates every access request using identity, role, device trust, MFA, behavior, and anomaly signals.

- Produces allow, deny, or deception-routing decisions and models least privilege, RBAC, and owner-only vault boundaries.
- Routes suspicious sessions into decoy containment with structured audit logging and SOC-style visibility. No hack-back, no retaliation.
- Built with Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, and SQLite.

**Explore:** [View Project Labyrinth](https://github.com/AL91Cole/ai-guardian-assistant-project-labyrinth)

### 📚 AI Ethics and Moral Philosophy

A living research repository connecting moral philosophy with practical AI governance and security design.

- Develops my proposed framework of **bounded moral learning**: AI systems that can learn within explicit, human-set moral limits and must escalate rather than improvise beyond them.
- Examines human responsibility, concentrated power, dignity, consent, privacy, accessibility, accountability, appeal, and repair.
- Clearly separates my own philosophical proposals from established standards, and maps each principle onto Guardian systems, Zero Trust, and defensive deception.

**Explore:** [View AI Ethics and Moral Philosophy](https://github.com/AL91Cole/ai-ethics-and-moral-philosophy)

## How I think about AI

I'm optimistic about AI and skeptical of AI hype, which is why I keep building things that test the difference.

- **Local-first, explain-first.** Analysis should happen on the user's device whenever possible, and a warning should say *why* in words a non-expert understands.
- **AI must not become a place where humans hide responsibility.** Every automated decision I design has a named human owner, an audit trail, and a path to appeal.
- **Off by default.** Cloud inference, memory, analytics, cameras, and microphones all start off and require an informed, visible choice to turn on.
- **Teach the audit, not just the tool.** My AI literacy labs ask students to review prompts, claims, bias, and generated code, minimize data, and reject source injection, because the next generation needs to question AI, not just use it.
- **Protect and contain, never retaliate.** Defensive deception and Zero Trust are about safely saying no, not about striking back.
- **Use AI to build, with a human signature.** AI coding agents help me move faster on my own projects. I review every line, keep secrets out of every prompt, and run privacy checks in CI on every push.
